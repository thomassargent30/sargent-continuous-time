## How the index is organized

Entries appear under the term the book uses, with the qualifier as a subentry. Definitions of
the numbered Definitions and Theorems are indexed at the statement itself, not at the chapter
opening, so a locator takes the reader to the result rather than to the front of a chapter.

Names of authors appear only where a chapter reports that author's work.
